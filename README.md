# TukiApps

**Super Beneficial Only social app store + AI-powered app builder**  
Lives inside TukiLifeParadise • Omnibeneficencia 100/100

TukiApps is a platform where people share the apps they love, discover apps they might truly like, and can describe any beneficial app in natural language so the AI builds and publishes it.

## Vision

- Social discovery of beneficial apps
- Clean app store experience
- AI App Builder (describe → working app)
- Optional HulosChain ownership verification
- Deep integration with TukiLifeParadise (Wall Computers, Magias, materialization platforms)

## Core Pillars

1. **Social Feed** — Share apps you love with screenshots and reasons
2. **Smart Discovery** — “Apps you might like” based on real taste + beneficial impact
3. **App Store** — Browse, search, install, rate
4. **AI App Builder** — Natural language → live preview → publish
5. **Sovereignty Layer** — Optional HulosChain attestation for creators and apps
